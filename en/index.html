The decision engine

The smallest heal that lands in time

SmartHeal measures the damage per second each player takes and estimates the time until death. Then it picks the smallest heal that lands before that and still covers the damage taken during the cast.

SituationWhat lights up
Tank at 85%, light steady damage (1% HP/s)Renew / Rejuvenation. The HoT out-heals the damage, no mana wasted on a big heal.
Player at 70%, no incoming damageThe smallest rank that tops them up. No overheal.
Tank hit for 70% of their health in one blowFlash Heal + Shield (Priest) or Regrowth + Swiftmend (Druid). Slow heals would land too late.
Your Renew is already tickingNo HoT suggestion until it expires.
Not enough mana for a rankThe best heal you can still pay for. Icons you cannot afford get a blue veil, like the game's action bars.
You drop below 30% manaMana-saving mode: smaller ranks, emergency heals only when truly needed.
Features

Everything on the bar, and around it

Everything on the bar

  • Incoming heals, yours and other healers'
  • Remaining HoT healing, shrinking as ticks land
  • Absorb shields and your buffs with timers
  • Damage per second in red, row dims out of range
  • Five-second-rule bar for mana regeneration

Hover keys

Hover a row and press 1 2 3… to cast the matching icon on that player. Move the mouse away and your action bar keys are back. Active only in a group by default, so solo play is never affected.

Party messages

Automatic, editable messages when you go out of mana, pull aggro, take burst damage, drink or resurrect someone. Each has a Test button that previews it only to you.

Gear upgrade alerts

Loot an item that beats what you wear and a pop-up shows the gain and the stat changes, with Equip, Ignore and Close. Only items your character can really wear, weighted by your spec and talents.

Your real numbers

SmartHeal reads your spellbook and the healing value of every rank from its tooltip, so talents and gear are included. New ranks appear as you learn them.

How to use it

From download to your first heal

  1. Install

    Download from CurseForge and extract into World of Warcraft/<your Forever folder>/Interface/AddOns/. The folder must be named SmartHeal.

  2. Open the options

    Type /sh, right-click the frame, or left-click the moon on your minimap. Pick your language at the top right, or leave it on Automatic.

  3. Check your spells

    In the Spells tab every healing rank you know is listed with its value and cast time. Untick any rank you never want suggested.

  4. Join a group and heal

    Click the glowing icon, or hover the row and press its number. The suggestion updates as damage comes in.

  5. Tune it if you want

    • Algorithm: reaction time, safety margin, stability
    • Messages: edit or switch off each party message
    • Gear: stat profile and minimum gain for alerts

Goals

Four ways to choose

Items

Quest rewards your character can actually wear, grouped by quest and sorted by level, with the stats and the gain over what you wear now. The best choice of each quest for your role is highlighted in a discreet green, and the addon asks before you add something weaker than what you wear. Dungeon rewards are listed too, marked in red.

Professions

Herbalism, Alchemy or Cooking up to 225, in every Alliance zone. The route takes you to the nearest trainer, to the next rank at the right time, and to every reagent, vendor or herb you need.

Long chains

Quest chains of five or more quests in your zones, longest first. Tick one to add the whole chain.

Secret

Hidden Forever quests, such as the Cozy Sleeping Bag chain (+3% XP while resting) or Silver of the Waves.

Gear is scored for your class and specialisation, weapon damage included. Weapons you have not learned yet are flagged, and when your bags hold something better than what you wear, a pop-up offers to equip it.

How to use it

Create, change and follow a route

  1. Install and open

    Extract into Interface/AddOns/ as a folder named LunasParcours. Open with the minimap button or /parcours.

  2. Choose where to search

    The zone you are in is always included. Press Zones… to tick other zones and plan ahead.

  3. Pick your goals

    • Items: one reward per quest; green "+3.2" means an upgrade
    • Professions: tick one, then a target like 75 / 150 / 225
    • Long chains and Secret: tick to add, hover for details

  4. Read the preview

    The box under the list sums up the plan, for example 6 quests, 18 steps · 2 bonuses. Press Add to take free bonus items on the same route.

  5. Launch and follow

    Press Create route. The step card tells you what to do now; most steps complete by themselves. Use Done, Skip and Back for the rest. You are after lists all your goals in the order you will get them, with the steps left for each.

  6. Change it any time

    Tick or untick goals and press Create route again. Quests you already accepted or finished stay done.

Who it is for

Made for two kinds of players

Completionists

You want the whole story of every zone, grey quests included, not just the fastest XP. Every doable quest is on the list, nothing disappears silently, and a progress bar for every zone and profession shows how complete your journey really is.

Busy parents

You have an hour after the kids are in bed and you don't want to spend it on a quest website. The shortest route is already planned, you can stop and resume at any time, even days later, and the arrow takes you there.

Features

The whole journey, at a glance

Journey tab

Your zones with a progress bar: done, skipped by you, justified, waiting for level, to do. Press Start the journey and the shortest route is built with every quest you can do now. It is recalculated at every level.

Every skip has a reason

Skip asks why: elite or group, dungeon, reputation, broken or not found, my choice. Other quests are justified automatically, for example a profession you have not learned. You can restore or add them back at any time.

Gear tab

Every quest reward your character can wear, with its gain over what you wear now. The weights behind the score are shown openly. The best choice for your role is highlighted in a discreet green. Tick an item and its quest chain moves to the front of the route, which recalculates by itself. The items you ticked are listed at the top of the route, with the steps left until you get them.

Easier in a group

A [2+] tag marks quests that are easier with friends: the game's group tag, elite enemies, or many enemies around the objective. Elite quests stay on your journey; you decide.

Professions

Cooking, Fishing and First Aid are always part of the journey. Every other profession joins as soon as you learn it, and quests that need a higher skill wait until you reach it.

With the other Luna addons

Install it next to Luna's Parcours and SmartHeal LindaLuna. Settings and arrow stay separate, and gear alerts and Luna's farewell are never doubled. Luna's Journey is for doing everything; for a few items, a profession or a long chain, Luna's Parcours is the right tool.

How to use it

From the first zone to the last quest

  1. Install and open

    Extract into Interface/AddOns/ as a folder named LunasJourney. Open with the minimap button (the moon and the paw prints), /journey or /lj.

  2. Check your zones

    In the Journey tab the zone you are in is always included. Use Zones… to plan ahead.

  3. Start the journey

    Press Start the journey. The Route tab opens with the step to do now and the next five.

  4. Follow the arrow

    Most steps complete by themselves. Use Skip (it asks why), Done or Back when needed.

  5. Pick your gear

    Open the Gear tab now and then and tick the items you want first. The route recalculates by itself. You can tick several items, one per quest.
